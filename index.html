Tutorials that check your work.

Miyagi turns any tutorial into a graded lab that runs beside your code. The video waits at each step until your own code, terminal or training run passes that step's check.

How it works

  1. AskSay what you want to learn.
  2. FindMiyagi picks a strong tutorial and splits it into steps.
  3. BuildYou follow along in your own editor and terminal. Each step has a real check.
  4. PassOff track? A coach card shows what went wrong and where to look. Pass, and the video moves on.
