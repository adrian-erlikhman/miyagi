questions.

    

      
what is miyagi?
an app that turns any tutorial into a lesson you do. the video waits until you've done each step, and miyagi checks it.

      
which apps does it work with?
anything on your screen. vs code, jupyter and your terminal get deeper checks, since miyagi can run your code.

      
is it watching my screen all the time?
no. it only looks during a lesson, to check your step. those screenshots go to anthropic's claude, the ai that checks your work, so close private apps first.

      
what do i need?
a mac with apple silicon on macos 13 or later, for now. windows comes later.

      
how do i get it?
it's coming soon for public access. join the waitlist and we'll email you when it opens. have a code? enter it at the top of the page.
